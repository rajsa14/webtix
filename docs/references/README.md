# Reference sheet

Captured 10 Oct 2026 with Playwright (Chromium): 1280×800, and 400×860 as a phone (touch, 2× pixels).
`-scrolled` is the same 1280 page after one wheel scroll of 1400 px. Third-party screenshots, internal reference only: do not publish them or ship them with the site.

For each site: what we take, as decided for the redesign (October 2026), then two lines for a small Prague business.

---

## itsoffbrand.com: type scale only

| 1280 | 1280 scrolled | 400 |
|---|---|---|
| ![](itsoffbrand.com-1280.jpg) | ![](itsoffbrand.com-1280-scrolled.jpg) | ![](itsoffbrand.com-400.jpg) |

- **Works:** three huge uppercase words fill the whole first screen and still read in one glance; the jump from tiny nav labels to giant display type is the whole effect, and it costs nothing.
- **Avoid:** the loader. After 6 seconds both widths still showed only the loading mark, and the hero only appeared on the second capture. A café owner on mobile data leaves before that. Also no abstract 3D blob: it says nothing about the offer.

## madebyhart.com: one physical hero object (our hanging sign)

| 1280 | 1280 scrolled | 400 |
|---|---|---|
| ![](madebyhart.com-1280.jpg) | ![](madebyhart.com-1280-scrolled.jpg) | ![](madebyhart.com-400.jpg) |

- **Works:** one real-looking object (the TV on a workbench) makes the studio feel crafted and physical, which fits a sign-painter workshop; our sign can play that role at a fraction of the weight.
- **Avoid:** on a phone the object takes over half of the first screen; the only text is a long italic sentence, and there is no button and no offer. Our H1, price and button sit next to the sign, not after it. No logo wall either.

## studiomirage.io: one dark stage only

| 1280 | 1280 scrolled | 400 |
|---|---|---|
| ![](studiomirage.io-1280.jpg) | ![](studiomirage.io-1280-scrolled.jpg) | ![](studiomirage.io-400.jpg) |

- **Works:** a dark full-width stage turns one piece of content into an event; we use exactly one, for the "Z toho, co už máte" scene, and the page goes back to cream after it.
- **Avoid:** "Scroll to start exploring" as the whole first screen. On a phone there is no offer, no price and no headline, only the name. No sound, no gate, nothing blocking the first screen.

## studiors.be: huge headline with one italic word, numbered project list

| 1280 | 1280 scrolled | 400 |
|---|---|---|
| ![](studiors.be-1280.jpg) | ![](studiors.be-1280-scrolled.jpg) | ![](studiors.be-400.jpg) |

- **Works:** "STUDIO / *Créatif*" shows how one italic word gives a bold headline a voice. The numbered list (01 Azzato, 02 …) is the clearest way to show a small body of work, and the hard cut from dark to cream is a confident section change. We set the italic in Instrument Sans Italic 700, so we stay with one font family.
- **Avoid:** the serif italic (a second font family), the giant scrolling logo strip, and the "W. Nominee" badge on the edge. On the phone the dark 3D object sits behind the grey description text, which makes it hard to read.

## animaapp.com: hero input with two clear starting points

| 1280 | 1280 scrolled | 400 |
|---|---|---|
| ![](animaapp.com-1280.jpg) | ![](animaapp.com-1280-scrolled.jpg) | ![](animaapp.com-400.jpg) |

- **Works:** the input box in the hero is the call to action. You start by giving them something, which is exactly our "paste your web or Instagram address" idea.
- **Avoid:** the yellow banner above the nav, and the cookie bar. At 400 px the cookie bar covers the bottom quarter of the first screen and pushes the input below it. Ours stays small and never sits over the hero. Also avoid the long subtitle: theirs takes four lines on a phone, and ours is one sentence.
